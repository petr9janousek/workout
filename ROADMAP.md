# Roadmap

What we've agreed to build next. Done items move to "Features in detail" in [DOCUMENTATION.md](DOCUMENTATION.md).

## Next (v0.11): gym day
- **Other activities:** log a run, walk, ride, swim, dance, yoga, hike, team sport or anything else, with minutes, how hard it was, and optionally distance and calories (from a fitness tracker). Shown in the calendar in a second colour. Counts toward the weekly goal and streak when it's 20 minutes or more. "Active minutes this week" next to the WHO's 150
- **Swap an exercise** when the machine is taken: suggests exercises for the same muscles; changes only today's workout, not the plan

## Later
- **"Lose fat" focus:** 2–3 full-body strength days to keep muscle, an activity target (~250 min a week, per ACSM), the body weight trend, and a clear note that food decides most of the result. No calorie counting. Needs other activities first
- **Share a plan as a link** (the plan is stored inside the link; same data as the file)
- **Weekly goal optional** ("no goal")
- **A strength plan** (5×5 style) in the library
- **Mobility exercises** in the exercise library (timed holds), not in plans
- **Automatic backup** to the user's own Google Drive (optional)

## Ideas, not decided
- Two people on one phone

- Weekly summary, deload week suggestions
